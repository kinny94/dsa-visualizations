why no explicit LCA?

      Both paths start from the root, so they share a common prefix exactly up to the LCA. The first index where they diverge is the LCA's children branch. You never need to name the LCA — strip the shared prefix and you've found it implicitly.
    

    

      
why "U" instead of the real L/R?

      Going up, the character we appended going down doesn't matter — we're just going to the parent. All start-side chars become 'U'.
