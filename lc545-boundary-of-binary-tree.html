(0) result.add(root); if root is leaf, stop.

      
(1) addLeftBoundary(root.left):

      
while !null && !leaf: add; prefer left else right

      
(2) addLeaves(root):

      
DFS; if leaf, add. recurse left then right.

      
(3) addRightBoundary(root.right):

      
walk prefer right else left, skip leaves, then reverse the appended segment.

why skip leaves in 1 & 3

      Phase 2 catches every leaf. If phase 1 or 3 also included them we’d get duplicates. So the two boundary walks stop AT (not through) the first leaf.
