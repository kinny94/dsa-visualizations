    The key move
    
      At each polled node, look at node.left. If it exists AND it’s a leaf (both its children are null), that’s a left leaf — add its val and DON’T offer it. Otherwise offer it. Right children are always just offered. This “check at parent” trick solves the “am I a left child?” question that a leaf can’t answer by itself.
