Three cases

    

      
node.val < low

      
Left subtree is all < val < low → SKIP LEFT. Only right subtree could contribute.

      
node.val > high

      
Right subtree is all > val > high → SKIP RIGHT. Only left subtree could contribute.

      
low ≤ node.val ≤ high

      
Include val. Recurse BOTH sides — both may have in-range nodes.

    

    

      
Why pruning doesn't always help

      

        We still need to visit every in-range node to add it. Pruning only skips OUT-of-range nodes. Worst case (range covers everything) is O(n); tight bound is O(H + k) where k = nodes in range.
