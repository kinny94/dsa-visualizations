Why the dummy

      Instead of branching on “is this the first child I’ve placed this level?” we start tail = dummy. Every subsequent tail.next = child works identically whether it’s the first or twentieth. When the inner loop ends, dummy.next IS the next level’s leftmost — drop down with cur = dummy.next.
